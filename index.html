About

Performance Is a Nervous System Output.

Athletic performance is a nervous system output. Sleep deprivation, chronic travel stress, facility design, and organizational culture are not soft factors — they are inputs to a biological system that produces wins, recoveries, and careers. Governing them intentionally is a performance advantage.

NSAG for Sport adapts the 14-dimension Nervous-System-Aware Governance framework to the specific pressures of athletic institutions: college programs, professional teams, performance facilities, and governing bodies. The assessment produces a prioritized roadmap — not a wellness program.

The first pitch targets are already identified. The pipeline includes F1 and the LA28 Olympics. The framework exists. What's missing is the institutional will to treat athlete nervous system governance as a governance question, not a coaching one.
