03 / Voice without retaliation

Participation and data dignity

Design athlete input so opting out, reporting concerns, or disagreeing does not threaten selection, playing time, scholarship, employment, or care.

Do not convert care into surveillance. Collect the minimum data needed for a defined purpose; separate welfare support from punitive selection decisions; offer meaningful alternatives; and use qualified privacy, labor, safeguarding, and medical review.
