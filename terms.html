Terms and boundaries

Effective August 16, 2026.

This tool supports organizational inquiry. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, clear participation, determine safeguarding or legal compliance, or guarantee welfare or performance.

Scores and roadmaps are planning prompts, not clinical conclusions, certifications, risk clearances, or standards of care. Qualified sports medicine, mental-health, safeguarding, legal/labor, privacy, accessibility, education, and performance professionals retain responsibility.

Organizations remain responsible for emergency response, athlete consent and non-retaliation, minors, accessibility, collective agreements, privacy, independent care, current-law analysis, evidence verification, and decisions. Do not use this tool for emergencies.

The tool is provided as-is and may change. Maintain exports of important work.
